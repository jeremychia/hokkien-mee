How ratings work
Ratings are derived from the language used in posts and comments. Positive signals (e.g. "shiok", "best", "worth the queue") and negative signals (e.g. "bland", "disappointing", "avoid") are tallied to produce a 1–5 star score. A location needs at least 2 reviews with clear sentiment to receive a rating. Ratings are indicative — they reflect group members' opinions, not an official review.
